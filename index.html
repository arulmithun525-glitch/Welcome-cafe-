Who We Are

More Than
a Café

Welcome to Welcome Cafee — your go-to destination for delicious and mouth-watering treats in Kosakulam, Madurai. Our café is a warm and inviting space, perfect for catching up with friends or enjoying a cozy afternoon alone.

With a welcoming atmosphere and friendly staff, we strive to make every visit a memorable one. Our menu features a fusion of Indian and Western flavours that will surely tantalize your taste buds — from crunchy and spicy to sweet and refreshing.
